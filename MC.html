What's in the Modpack (15 Mods)

This pack keeps gameplay 100% vanilla—no new blocks, items, or machines—while adding proximity voice chat, massive view distance, shaders, and FPS boosts on our Fabric 26.2 server:

  • Proximity Voice Chat (Simple Voice Chat): Talk to nearby players right inside Minecraft—voices get louder as you walk closer and fade as you move away. Press V in-game to pick your mic/speakers or toggle Push-to-Talk.
  • Massive View Distance (Distant Horizons): Lets you see mountains and landscapes far beyond normal Minecraft render distance without lagging your PC. Give it a minute to cache distant chunks as you explore.
  • Shaders Ready (Iris Shaders): Adds shader support under Options > Video Settings > Shader Packs. You can drop shader zip files in or toggle shaders off anytime if you want maximum FPS.
  • Major FPS & Memory Boosts (Sodium, Sodium Extra, Reese's Sodium Options, Lithium, FerriteCore, C2ME, Fast Noise, Structure Layout Optimizer): Speeds up chunk loading, reduces RAM usage, and organizes your Video Settings menu cleanly.
  • Helper & Menu Mods (Mod Menu, Crash Assistant, Fabric API, Resourceful Config): Behind-the-scenes libraries and menus so mods load cleanly and tell you what happened if the game ever crashes.

Step-by-Step Setup Guide

Before You Start: Account & Game

  1. You must already have a Microsoft account (older Mojang accounts must be migrated to Microsoft).
  2. You must already have purchased Minecraft (specifically Minecraft: Java Edition) on that Microsoft account.
  3. Send your exact Minecraft username so I can add you to the server whitelist.

Step 1: Download & Install CurseForge

  1. Click Download CurseForge above and download the CurseForge app installer for your computer.
  2. Run the installer and follow the prompts to finish setup.
  3. Open CurseForge, pick Minecraft, select the recommended/standard setup, and click Continue.

Step 2: Download & Import Our Family Modpack (.zip)

  1. Click the blue Download Family Modpack (.zip) button at the top of this page to save Distant Horizons & Iris Shaders-0.4.2+26.2.fabric.zip to your Downloads folder (keep it zipped—do not unzip it).
  2. In the CurseForge app, go to Minecraft > My Modpacks and click Create Custom Profile (or the Import button at the top).
  3. Choose Import (or "import a previously created profile"), select the downloaded .zip file, and wait for CurseForge to automatically download all 15 Fabric 26.2 mods.

Step 3: Launch the Game & Set Up Voice Chat

  1. Hover over the imported modpack in My Modpacks and click Play.
  2. When the Minecraft Launcher opens, sign in with your Microsoft account that owns Minecraft and click Play.
  3. Once in-game, press V to open the Simple Voice Chat menu, choose your microphone and speakers, and run a quick mic test.

Step 4: Join the World

  1. Click Multiplayer on the Minecraft main menu.
  2. Click Add Server (or Direct Connection).
  3. Click Copy on the server address above, paste dedication-wl.tun.ply.gg, save, and join the world.
